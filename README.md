# Habbine Estelle Kim — Legal research

Comparative legal research at the intersection of law, technology, economics, behavioural science and quantitative methods.
Avocate (Paris and New York bars) · Guest lecturer, Université Paris Cité and Aix-Marseille Université · EN / FR / KO

📄 All papers on SSRN: [author page](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=4745485)

## Publications

Click a title to read the full paper (PDF). Where available, the SSRN link gives the official version and citation.

### 2025

**[A Comparative Law Approach on the Conceptualisation of the Environmental Accountability Principle Towards International Environmental Justice](2025-environmental-accountability-principle.pdf.pdf)**
Why fragmented environmental rules let polluters shop for the most lenient jurisdiction, and how a common accountability principle could close the gap.

### 2024

**[The Conception of a Practical Legal & Compliance Tool Through Mathematical Modeling and Statistical Analysis: Global Environmental Impact & Efficiency Valuation Model](2024-environmental-impact-efficiency-valuation-model.pdf.pdf)**
An original mathematical model that turns legal, economic and policy factors into a single index to compare environmental impact and efficiency, and help detect misleading reporting.

### 2022

**[Decentralisation and Digital Transformation: User e-Consciousness and Accountability of Intermediators](2022-decentralisation-digital-transformation.pdf)** · co-authored with Maciej Bernard Plotka · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4256243)
Rather than one-size-fits-all rules for digital markets, this paper proposes user self-risk-assessment habits and incentives for intermediaries to act transparently.

**[Projet de sauvetage : le contrat à durée déterminée esportif](2022-esports-fixed-term-employment-contract.pdf)** (FR) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4125169)
An assessment of France's dedicated fixed-term employment contract for esports players: what works, what doesn't, and possible reforms.

**[Where Esport Law Does Not Exist](2022-where-esport-law-does-not-exist-EN.pdf)** (EN) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4047288)
**[Quand la loi de l'esport n'existe pas](2022-quand-la-loi-de-lesport-nexiste-pas-FR.pdf)** (FR) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4068893)
A comparison of French and European approaches to esport, and the alternative ways stakeholders' rights can be protected without dedicated legislation.

**[A Tax Perspective on the Enforcer's True Role in Onshore and Offshore Common Law Jurisdictions](2022-tax-enforcer-role-common-law-trusts.pdf)** · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=3972163)
How purpose trusts without beneficiaries, combined with corporate structures, enable aggressive tax planning, and what the enforcer's role really is.

### 2021

**[How Do Consumer-Focused Innovations in the Legal Services Market Invigorate Access to Justice?](2021-legal-services-innovation-access-to-justice.pdf)** · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=3972167)
How consumer empowerment and technologies such as AI, big data and online services are reshaping legal services and widening access to justice.

**[The Tech Tortoise, the Regulatory Hare and the Digital Race 2.0](2021-tech-tortoise-regulatory-hare.pdf)** (EN/FR) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=3907828)
A bilingual fable about regulators racing to keep up with blockchain, AI and the Internet of Things.
